# Recruiter Goose: Personality Card

**Look:** Bright Office (10/24/2026; replaced Espresso so each ADGE-Co app has its own look).
**One-line feel:** a clean shirt on the first day of an office job. It should pass the "is this as legit as LinkedIn or Indeed?" test at a glance.

## Colors (one meaning each)
| Token | Light | Dark | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper | #F6F5F1 | #0F1114 | Background |
| Raised | #FDFCF9 (never pure white) | #181B20 | Cards, sheets |
| Ink | #121417 | #F2F3F5 | Text |
| Secondary | #5F636B | #9A9FA8 | Supporting text |
| Hairline | #E4E1DA | #262A31 | Borders (hairlines, not shadows) |
| Ultramarine | #2E44D9, 3px lip #1F2FA3 | #4F63F0 / text #8E9BFF | The main action and the selected tab only |
| Fresh green | #177A43 text, #1F9D55 fill | #4CC985 | Postings open under 72 hours, nothing else |
| Amber | #8A5300 | #E8B65C | Check-first warnings |
| Red | #B0322A | #F0938A | Real problems only |

Company tiles get one muted color each, picked from the company name (8 tones).

## Type
- Titles: Geist 700-800, tracked tight (-2.5% to -3.5%).
- Body: Geist 400-600.
- Numbers (fit counts, celebration numbers, follow-up dates): Manrope 800 with lining, even-width digits.

## Shape and light
- Corners 16 outer, 12 inner. One soft light from the top. Frosted light glass on the tab bar only; sheets and cards are solid.

## Mascot
The Recruiter Goose: sunglasses, headset, badge on a lanyard, now in an ultramarine quarter-zip (recolored from the Espresso brown jacket, same art). Poses: wave = welcome, papers = empty My Jobs, coffee = nothing due, cheer = interview or offer.

## Signature
Merit-style nudge cards at the top of My Jobs (a reply waiting, a follow-up due, a job gone quiet), each with one button.

## Reference apps
Merit (Dribbble app-job-mobile #02), LinkedIn, Indeed for the legit test; Linear for type tightness.

## Big moments
- Tier 2: applied (toast + follow-up set).
- Tier 3: interview from a reply.
- Tier 4: an offer (full-screen, big number, cheer pose).
